01What does design intent describe in a CAD model?

Design intent captures the functional relationships and expected behaviour of the model when it is edited.

02Which sketch constraint forces two line segments to meet at the same point?

A coincident constraint places two points at the same location, which can join line endpoints.

03A sketch can still be dragged after adding several dimensions. What is the best description?

If geometry can still move in an unintended way, at least one degree of freedom remains and the sketch is under-constrained.

04Why is it usually better to use symmetry instead of dimensioning both sides independently?

A symmetry relationship directly expresses intent and lets one change preserve both sides without competing dimensions.

05Which orthographic view should normally be chosen as the front view?

The principal view should explain the object clearly, show its characteristic form, and minimise hidden detail where practical.

06What makes a feature reference more robust?

Origins, primary planes, and intentional datums are less likely to disappear or be renumbered when surrounding geometry changes.
