Construction

Two cubic bases, one surface

\[\mathbf r(u,v)=\sum_{i=0}^{3}\sum_{j=0}^{3}N_{4,i}(u)M_{4,j}(v)\mathbf P_{ij},\qquad 0\le u,v\le1.\]

Following the notes, the first basis index denotes order. Both directions have degree 3 and order 4, with active parameter span [0, 1]. Increasing i follows u; increasing j follows v. The geometric coordinates x, y, z are separate from the surface parameters.

Clamped: \(U=V=\{0,0,0,0,1,1,1,1\}\). Repeating each end knot four times makes this single patch a bicubic Bézier patch. Its one-direction weights are \((1-t)^3,\ 3t(1-t)^2,\ 3t^2(1-t),\ t^3\). The surface interpolates the four corner controls; its boundary curves generally do not pass through the other edge controls.

Open (unclamped): \(U=V=\{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4\}\). Each direction uses the uniform cubic blending functions. The patch generally does not interpolate its corner controls. Here “open” means unclamped; neither mode wraps into a periodic surface.

In both modes, products of the one-direction basis values give the 16 weights and keep the patch within the convex hull of the control points. Switching modes preserves every control point.
