Construction

Two cubic bases, one surface

\[\mathbf r(u,v)=\sum_{i=0}^{3}\sum_{j=0}^{3}N_{4,i}(u)M_{4,j}(v)\mathbf P_{ij},\qquad 0\le u,v\le1.\]

Following the notes, the first basis index denotes order. Both directions have degree 3 and order 4, with uniform knot vectors \(U=V=\{-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4\}\). Their single active span is [0, 1]. The geometric coordinates x, y, z are separate from the surface parameters u and v.

Each direction uses the four uniform cubic blending functions. Their products give the 16 weights. Increasing i follows the u direction; increasing j follows v.

A uniform B-spline patch lies within the convex hull of the control points and generally does not interpolate its corner points. It is not a periodic surface. With this fixed 4 × 4 net, all 16 controls influence the interior of the single patch.
