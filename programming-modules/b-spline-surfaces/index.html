Construction

Two B-spline bases, one surface

\[\mathbf r(u,v)=\sum_{i=0}^{c-1}\sum_{j=0}^{c-1}N_{m,i}(u)M_{m,j}(v)\mathbf P_{ij},\qquad 0\le u,v\le1.\]

Following the notes, the first basis index denotes order \(m\), and the degree is \(n=m-1\). Here \(c\) is the number of control points per direction (4, 5 or 6), giving \(c^2\) points in total. Both directions use the selected order. Increasing i follows u; increasing j follows v. The geometric coordinates x, y, z are separate from the surface parameters.

Each knot vector contains \(c+m\) entries. The active interval is \([U_{m-1},U_c]=[0,1]\), with \(c-m+1\) nonzero spans in each direction. Basis values are computed using the Cox–de Boor recursion, including the right endpoint.

Clamped: each end knot is repeated \(m\) times, with uniformly spaced interior knots. The surface interpolates the four corner controls; its boundary curves generally do not pass through the other edge controls. When \(c=m\), the surface is a single Bézier patch. The default 4×4 grid with order 4 is bicubic, with \(U=V=\{0,0,0,0,1,1,1,1\}\).

Open (unclamped): all knots are uniformly spaced, without repeated ends: \(U_i=V_i=(i-m+1)/(c-m+1)\), for \(i=0,\ldots,c+m-1\). The surface generally does not interpolate its corner controls. Here “open” means unclamped; neither mode wraps into a periodic surface.

In both modes, products of the one-direction basis values give the \(c^2\) weights and keep the surface within the convex hull of the control points. Changing order or boundary condition preserves every control point.
