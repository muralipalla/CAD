Course learning outcomes

What you will be able to do

By completing the theory, programming activities, and self-assessment, you should be able to:

  • Transform and project geometryApply homogeneous transformations, rotations, projections, and coordinate-frame reasoning in two and three dimensions.
  • Analyze curves and surfacesInterpret derivatives, arc length, curvature, torsion, local frames, surface normals, and continuity.
  • Construct design geometryEvaluate and create Hermite, Bézier, B-spline, NURBS, tensor-product, and Coons representations.
  • Reason about solid modelsCompare B-Rep and CSG, distinguish geometry from topology, and explain half-edge structures and Euler operations.
  • Select tools and exchange dataMatch CAD, meshing, and visualization software—and native, exact, mesh, drawing, or lightweight formats—to a downstream task.
  • Communicate product definitionExplain a controlled CAD workflow and use GD&T, PMI, and model-based definition to preserve design intent.
  • Implement and check algorithmsTranslate geometric methods into Python, MATLAB, and interactive JavaScript, then validate numerical and visual results.
